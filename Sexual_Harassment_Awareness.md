# Sexual Harassment Awareness

## 1. What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment includes any unwelcome behaviour of a sexual nature that makes a person feel uncomfortable, offended, humiliated, unsafe, or intimidated. It can happen in workplaces, educational institutions, public places, or online environments.

### Common behaviours that may cause sexual harassment

#### Verbal Behaviour
- Making inappropriate comments about someone's appearance or body
- Sexual jokes, remarks, or teasing

#### Non-Verbal Behaviour
- Staring in an uncomfortable or inappropriate manner
- Making gestures with sexual meaning

#### Physical Behaviour
- Unwanted touching, hugging, or patting
- Attempting physical contact without consent

#### Online / Digital Harassment
- Sending unwanted messages or explicit content through social media or chat platforms
- Sharing private images or information without permission



# 2. What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

If I face or witness sexual harassment, I would take responsible and appropriate action to ensure safety and support.

## Steps to Take

### If I Face Sexual Harassment

1. **Stay Calm and Prioritize Safety**
   - Move away from the unsafe situation if possible.
   - Seek help from trusted people nearby.

2. **Clearly Communicate Discomfort**
   - Politely but firmly tell the person that the behaviour is unwelcome.
   - Ask them to stop immediately.

3. **Document the Incident**
   - Note the date, time, location, and details of the incident.
   - Save messages, emails, screenshots, or any evidence if applicable.

4. **Report the Incident**
   - Inform a supervisor, HR department, teacher, Internal Complaints Committee (ICC), or relevant authority.
   - Use official complaint mechanisms available in the organization or institution.

5. **Seek Support**
   - Talk to trusted friends, family members, mentors, or counselors.
   - Seek legal or professional help if required.



## If I Witness Sexual Harassment

1. **Support the Victim**
   - Check if the person is safe and okay.
   - Listen respectfully without blaming or judging them.

2. **Do Not Ignore the Situation**
   - If safe, intervene appropriately or distract the harasser.
   - Encourage the victim to report the incident.

3. **Report the Behaviour**
   - Inform the appropriate authority or management if necessary.
   - Provide truthful information if asked as a witness.

4. **Promote a Respectful Environment**
   - Encourage awareness, equality, and respectful communication.
   - Avoid jokes or behaviour that may encourage harassment.
